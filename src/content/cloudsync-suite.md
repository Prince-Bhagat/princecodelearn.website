BMC Software's AMI zAdviser works on enhancing software delivery via contextual data capture. It uses DORA metrics and mainframe KPIs for insights, enabling performance benchmarking and development efficiency boost through AI recommendations.

The project involved leading Jira integration, collaborating with stakeholders to gather requirements, and managing interns. We increased deployment speed by automating build, test, and deploy with Jenkins CI/CD pipeline.

Tech Stack: NodeJS, Elasticsearch, Kibana, OpenSearch, AWS.
